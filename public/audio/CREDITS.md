# Om recitation

`om-tito-dutta.ogg` is the unchanged “Om pro.ogg” by Tito Dutta / Titodutta, own work dated 5 October 2011.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Om_pro.ogg

Original audio: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/9/94/Om_pro.ogg

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

It is a short Om/Aum pronunciation recording, not a full bhajan or long chanting performance. The optional browser arrangement adds silence between repetitions and changes playback gain; that audio arrangement is also offered under CC BY-SA 3.0. No endorsement is implied. Original browser-synthesized ambience and bell remain separate.
